Cesium ion credentials are stored locally in Chrome and are never written into source files.

Connect Cesium ion

  1. Create or sign in to a Cesium ion account.
  2. Copy an access token from Access Tokens with access to World Terrain, World Imagery and asset 96188.
  3. Paste the token above and save. Open a new tab to reconnect.
